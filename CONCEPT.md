# Concept of **Hunt of the Cult**

## Story

- Protagonist finds an architect of a Devil Worshipper Cult
- This cult finds out
- They follow him to his home
- They try getting in in order to sacrifice him
- First they try to instill fear into him, bait him into going outside
- After unsuccessful attempts, they try breaking in
- The Protagonist sees how his fiancée gets killed
- He then has to hide
- Afterwards, he has to flee and call the police
